Capybara Stack Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026   Updated Date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("this Policy") is provided by Xiao Ting ("we", "us"), and explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when operating the casual mini game Capybara Stack ("the Game"), as well as the rights you are entitled to.

Please read and fully understand this Policy before using the Game. By starting to use the Game, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Policy. The Game is a single-player casual game and does not provide account registration or login. We do not actively collect sensitive personal information that can directly identify you, such as your name, phone number, or ID number.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

To ensure the normal operation and basic experience of the Game, we process relevant information in the following ways:

1.1 Device Information (for screen adaptation and safety)

When you use the Game, we obtain information such as your device brand, device model, operating system and version, platform type, system language, screen resolution, pixel ratio, and safe area through the system interfaces of the platform. This information is used solely for screen adaptation, safe-area display, troubleshooting, and security of the Game, and is not used to identify you personally.

1.2 Local Storage Information (for saving game progress and preferences)

The Game stores the following information locally on your device: your high score, level unlock and completion progress, tutorial flag, and music and sound-effect settings. This information is kept only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers; it is mainly used to record your game progress and preferences. Uninstalling the Game or clearing local data will delete the above information.

1.3 Rewarded Video Ads (third-party advertising service)

The Game provides rewarded video features such as "revive via ad" and "double score via ad". These ads are served by the official advertising service of your distribution platform or its partner advertising platforms. When you voluntarily choose to watch an ad, the third-party advertising service provider may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, device model, operating system, and ad impression and interaction records, for the purposes of ad delivery, attribution analytics, and anti-fraud. We do not obtain additional personal information about you as a result of you watching ads.

1.4 Log Information

While you use the Game, the platform or third-party services may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, access time, and crash logs to ensure service security and diagnose issues.

2. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure of Information

We will not sell your personal information to any third party. Except in the following circumstances, we will not share, transfer, or publicly disclose your information:

  • third-party advertising service providers necessary for features such as rewarded video ads (see above);
  • mandatory requirements of laws and regulations or judicial and administrative authorities;
  • when necessary to protect our legitimate rights and interests or those of users (e.g., preventing fraud and handling security incidents).

3. Storage and Protection of Information

Your game progress and preference information is mainly stored locally on your device; information processed by third-party services is stored by the corresponding service providers under their own privacy policies. We take reasonable technical and managerial measures to protect the security of your information and prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, tampering, or loss. We retain relevant information only for the period reasonably necessary to achieve the stated purpose, after which it will be deleted or anonymized.

4. Cross-border Data Processing (for overseas distribution)

The Game may be provided outside your country or region through the distribution platform, and the relevant platforms or advertising service providers may process information overseas. We will require the relevant parties to take adequate protection measures in accordance with applicable data protection laws (such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)) to safeguard your information.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable laws and regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • to access the information we hold about you;
  • to correct or supplement inaccurate information;
  • to delete your information or withdraw previously granted consent;
  • to request the restriction or cessation of processing of your information;
  • to file a complaint about personal information processing with the relevant regulatory authority.

Because the Game has no account system and information is mainly stored locally, you can delete relevant information by uninstalling the Game or clearing local data. To exercise any of the other rights above, you may contact us using the information at the end of this Policy, and we will respond within a reasonable period.

6. Protection of Minors

The age rating of the Game is 8 years and above. We attach great importance to the protection of minors' information. If you are a minor, please use the Game under the guidance and consent of your guardian. We do not actively collect minors' personal information; if a guardian finds that we have collected a minor's information without valid consent, they may contact us using the information below, and we will promptly delete the relevant information.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Policy from time to time. When material changes are made, we will notify you through appropriate means. We recommend that you review this Policy periodically to stay informed of the latest version.
